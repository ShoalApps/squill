# Squill

Jot reminders down like ink on paper. A small Material You reminders app for Android, part of the Shoal apps.

- One-off, daily and weekly reminders that ring at the exact minute, even in Doze
- Done and Snooze buttons on the notification
- Colours follow your wallpaper on Android 12+
- Everything stays on your phone: no account, no network, no tracking
